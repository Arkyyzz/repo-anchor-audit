# Code of conduct

Help people improve the tool and understand its limits. Be respectful, give actionable feedback, and assume that contributors have different experience levels and available time.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, publishing private information, and deliberate disruption are unacceptable. Critique code and ideas without attacking people. Respect a request to stop contact.

Maintainers may edit or remove disruptive content and limit participation when necessary. They should explain the reason where practical, apply these expectations consistently, and avoid exposing private reports.

Before public launch, the maintainer must choose and publish a private reporting contact in this file. Until that contact exists, do not post private personal information in issues. For conduct occurring on GitHub, the platform's reporting tools remain available.

This policy applies in the project's issues, pull requests, discussions, and other spaces explicitly operated by its maintainers. It is an original short policy, not a claim of affiliation with a conduct-policy organization.
